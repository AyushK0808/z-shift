# Citation audit for `paper.tex`

Audited 2026-09-11. Scope: factual and technical claims in `paper/paper.tex`, the fit of every nearby external citation, and citation ranges that obscure which source supports which proposition. System design and benchmark results produced by this repository generally do not need external citations, but they still need traceable methods/data and appropriately narrow wording.

## What does not need an external citation

The architecture description (lines 254–362), benchmark numbers (lines 364–535), case-study timings (lines 537–607), and conclusions derived directly from those measurements do not require literature citations **if** the committed code/CSV/protocol is the declared evidence. For publication reproducibility, point readers to an archival repository/version and make the exact CSVs, scripts, environment, model/checkpoint identifiers, and hardware available. This is separate from adding literature citations and is especially important for the determinism and scaling claims.
